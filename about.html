About Wonderground

Wonderground is a small, independent browser arcade built for people who want to play something fun without a long setup. The site brings together quick games that are easy to understand and satisfying to replay, from score-chasing challenges to puzzles, timing tests, and playful action.

Our goal is to make discovering a new game feel as simple as opening a favorite playlist. The game feed is designed for quick sessions on a phone, tablet, or desktop, with a focused interface that keeps the game in the center. You can browse the collection, search for a title, save favorites, and jump back into games you have already tried.

Wonderground does not require an account to play. If you choose to sign in with Google, optional account features can help keep saved games and progress available across devices. We aim to keep those features useful and unobtrusive while respecting your choices.

This project is continuously improved as new games, accessibility refinements, and performance updates are added. We welcome thoughtful feedback about broken games, confusing controls, or ideas that would make the arcade more enjoyable.
