Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Wonderground (“we,” “us,” or “our”) provides a browser game collection at Wonderground. This policy explains what information may be handled when you use the site, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information you choose to provide

You can play without an account. If you use Google sign-in, Firebase Authentication receives information needed to authenticate you, such as your Google account identifier, name, email address, and profile image when provided by Google. We use this information to identify your account and provide account features. Google’s own policies also apply to its services.

Firebase and saved games

Wonderground uses Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Firestore, to support signed-in accounts and saved game data. Saved games, favorites, and related progress may be associated with your account so they can be available across devices. We do not sell this information. Firebase may process data on our behalf under its service terms and privacy protections.

Local storage

Whether or not you sign in, your browser may store preferences, recently played games, favorites, play counts, and similar settings using localStorage. This information stays in your browser and helps the site remember your choices. You can remove it through your browser’s site-data or storage settings. Clearing it may reset local preferences and saved local progress.

Advertising and cookies

We may use Google AdSense to show advertising. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to personalize or measure ads, limit repeated ads, and understand advertising performance. You can learn how Google uses advertising information at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Depending on your location, consent choices and browser controls may affect personalized advertising. You can manage cookies through your browser settings and Google’s available ad settings.

Security, retention, and children

We use reasonable measures and the settings offered by our service providers to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. We retain account and saved-game information while it is needed to provide the service or comply with obligations. Wonderground is intended for a general audience and is not knowingly directed to children under 13.

Your questions

For privacy questions or requests about your information, contact tencometstudios@gmail.com. We may update this policy when the site or applicable requirements change; the date above identifies the current version.
